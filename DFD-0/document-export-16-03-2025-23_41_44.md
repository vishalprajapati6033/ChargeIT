# DFD Level-0

### **Entities:**
1. **User**: An electric vehicle owner who interacts with the app for station search and booking.
2. **Admin**: Manages the system by controlling station and user data.
3. **Station Owner**: Manages station information and handles booking requests.
### **Data Flow:**
- **User to EV Charging Stations App:**
    - Sends **ID and Password** for login.
    - Requests to **find stations** and **book stations**.
    - Receives **confirmation of booking**.
    - Can view **profile information** and **booking/cancellation details**.
- **Admin to EV Charging Stations App:**
    - Sends **ID and Password** for login.
    - Manages **station information** and **user data**.
    - Can view the **stations list**, **booking list**, and **EV user details**.
- **Station Owner to EV Charging Stations App:**
    - Sends **ID and Password** for login.
    - Can **confirm/cancel bookings**.
    - Views **EV owner details** and **booking requests**.
    - Manages **profile information**.
### **Central Process (EV Charging Stations App):**
- Manages all data related to users, station owners, and bookings.
- Handles data flow between the users, admin, and station owners.
- Maintains databases for user information, booking requests, and station details.
